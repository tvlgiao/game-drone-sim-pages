Drone Sim

Press kit · Cowork Game Studio · contact@coworkgamestudio.com

Drone Sim is an FPV drone racing simulator with a real flight model. Race friends in live online rooms, chase ghosts on the leaderboards or race bots across eight courses, from a neon night loft to Ha Long Bay and Hoi An by night. It runs in any browser, on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and on Meta Quest.

Features

  • Flight model: 6-DOF rigid-body physics at a 1 kHz fixed step with a Betaflight-style flight controller; GPS (self-arming, height hold), Angle and Acro modes; editable per-axis rates and presets; stick modes 1–4
  • Cameras: FPV (adjustable camera tilt and field of view), line of sight from the pilot's spot, and chase
  • Eight courses: Training Field, Night Loft, Toy Town, Hoi An by Night, Ha Long Bay, City, Alpine Valley and Infinite World (seeded, shareable code); Full and Sprint routes on most courses; time of day outdoors
  • Modes: race against bots (five skill levels), Time Trial against your best ghost, Free Fly, Online
  • Online: live rooms for 2–8 pilots by room code or Quick match, bots fill empty seats; per-course leaderboards; race any listed pilot's ghost; “Challenge a friend” ghost links
  • Six airframes: from a 32 g tiny whoop to a 7" long-range quad; Workshop liveries and shareable build codes
  • Controls: touch twin sticks, Bluetooth and USB gamepads, keyboard and mouse, Meta Quest Touch controllers in VR
  • Ads (App Store version): only between races, never during flight
