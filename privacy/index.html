Privacy Policy

Drone Sim · Cowork Game Studio (COWORK COMPANY LIMITED) · Effective 12 October 2026

Summary: We do not collect personal data ourselves and there are no accounts. The free versions (iPhone, iPad, Android and the web) show ads served by Google, which uses device identifiers and cookies as described below; you can buy Remove Ads to turn them off. The paid Meta Quest app has no ads. Your settings and records stay on your device.

Who we are

Drone Sim ("the app") is an FPV drone flight simulator published by Cowork Game Studio ("we", "us") for iPhone, iPad, Android, Meta Quest and web browsers at dronesim.coworkgamestudio.com.

Data we collect

None unless you join the online leaderboards (below). The app has no analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs and does not ask you to sign in. The free versions contain Google's advertising SDKs (below), which collect data for ads under Google's policies.

Online leaderboards (optional)

Leaderboards and racing other pilots' ghosts are off until you open a leaderboard and choose a pilot name. From then on, each race you finish is sent to our server, run on Cloudflare Workers:

  • What is sent: your pilot name, a random pilot ID created on your device (no account, no email), the course, your lap time and ring split times, the kind of controls you used (touch, keyboard, gamepad or VR), and for a new personal best the recording of that lap (the drone's position and attitude, the "ghost"). Our server also records your country, derived by Cloudflare from your IP address; the IP address itself is not stored.
  • Who sees it: every player sees the leaderboard: pilot name, country, controls and lap time, and can race your best-lap ghost.
  • Why: to show leaderboards, let players race each other's ghosts, and remove cheating and abusive names. Reports you make about other pilots' names are kept for review.
  • Rules: no abusive, hateful or impersonating names. Such names are removed and their pilots banned. Use Report next to a name to flag it; reported pilots are hidden on your device at once.
  • Retention and deletion: leaderboard entries stay while the game runs online; pilots inactive for 24 months are deleted. To delete your pilot and all its laps now, email us your pilot name (see Contact).

Advertising (free versions)

  • iPhone, iPad and Android apps use Google AdMob to show a full-screen ad now and then between races (never during a flight). AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS only if you allow tracking, Android advertising ID), IP address, coarse location derived from it, device and app information and ad interactions, to serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Tracking permission (iOS): the app asks through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt whether ads may use your IDFA. Ads still show if you decline, just less personalised. You can change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before personalised ads, a Google consent message (a certified consent platform) asks for your choice. Reopen it any time from Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • Web version: may show ads served by Google AdSense, which uses cookies. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites; you can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
  • See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details; the store tells the app only whether you own Remove Ads, and the app remembers that on your device so ads stay off offline. Restore purchases in Settings brings it back on a new device.

Device features the app uses

  • Touch, keyboard and game controllers — read only to fly the drone, processed on the device.
  • Meta Quest headset and Touch controllers (VR mode) — head and controller position are used only to draw the view and fly the drone in real time. They are not recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Meta Quest account (Quest app only) — at launch the app asks the Meta Horizon Store on your headset whether your Meta account owns Drone Sim. The account ID it receives is checked on the device and immediately discarded: it is not stored, recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Audio output and vibration — for motor sound and rumble feedback. The app does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; ads shown to users identified as children are limited to non-personalised ads under Google's policies.

Third parties

Google (AdMob and AdSense, see Google Privacy Policy), the app stores (Apple App Store, Google Play, Meta Horizon Store) and the web host (GitHub Pages) process data such as ad requests, downloads, purchases or server logs under their own privacy policies. We receive only aggregated, anonymous reports from them (ad earnings, store statistics), never personal data.
